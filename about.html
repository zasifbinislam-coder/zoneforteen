Our Story

About Zone14

Built by fans, for fans

Zone14 was started by a small team of football obsessives in Dhaka who were tired of paying tourist prices for international jerseys — or worse, settling for low-quality knockoffs that fell apart after the first wash.

We work directly with manufacturers to source premium fan jerseys with the same fabric, fit, and crest accuracy as official stadium kits — at a fraction of the imported price. Every piece is inspected before it ships.

Our promise

  • Match-grade polyester · breathable mesh that holds up after dozens of washes
  • True-to-size fit aligned with Nike Stadium & Adidas Match charts
  • Fast delivery — 24 hours in Dhaka, 2–3 days nationwide
  • 7-day size exchange if you order the wrong fit
  • Best-price guarantee — find it cheaper elsewhere and we'll match it

How we got the name

The eighteen-yard box. The penalty area. The spot inside the box where you feel like a goal is inevitable — football's most dangerous real estate. We named ourselves after that feeling, because every fan deserves to wear the jersey that puts them in the zone.
