Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?+

Inside Dhaka: 24 hours. Outside Dhaka: 2–3 working days via Sundarban / Steadfast courier.

What payment methods do you accept?+

bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank transfer, and Cash on Delivery (COD) across Bangladesh.

Can I exchange or return my jersey?+

Yes — size exchange within 7 days of delivery. Item must be unworn with tags. Customized jerseys (name/number printed) are non-returnable.

Yes. Add ৳150 per jersey. Heat-pressed, durable, looks professional. Mention your name & number when ordering on WhatsApp.

These are premium-grade fan jerseys ("Master Copy") — same fabric, same fit, accurate crest prints. Not licensed originals, but the closest you can get for the price.

Do you ship outside Bangladesh?+

Not currently. We focus on serving the Bangladesh market with best prices and fast delivery.
