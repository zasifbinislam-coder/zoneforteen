We don't currently ship outside Bangladesh. If you're a Bangladeshi abroad ordering for family in BD, that works fine — just give us their address.

Damaged in transit

Inspect your package before paying the courier. If anything looks damaged, refuse the delivery and WhatsApp us a photo — we'll replace it free of charge.
